Diet & Lifestyle

Parhez Guide

Classical dietary and lifestyle guidance traditionally observed alongside homeopathic treatment.

Classical Antidote Avoidance (General Parhez)

Traditionally advised to avoid around the time of taking any remedy, believed by homeopaths to "antidote" (neutralise) its action - stated on virtually all major Indian brand labels (SBL, Schwabe India, Reckeweg, Adel):

  • Coffee and strong tea
  • Raw onion and garlic in excess
  • Camphor, menthol, and mint (including strongly minted toothpaste close to dosing time)
  • Strong perfumes and essential oils
  • Tobacco and alcohol

Practical Dosing Habits

  • Keep a 15-30 minute gap between taking a remedy and eating, drinking, or brushing teeth
  • Take the remedy on a clean tongue
  • Avoid touching pellets directly with your hand - tip into the cap or a spoon first
  • Store remedies away from direct sunlight, strong heat, and strongly-scented items
  • Keep the bottle cap on tightly when not in use

General Wellness Principles

Beyond remedy-specific antidote avoidance: adequate hydration, a diet with sufficient fresh fruit and vegetables and limited ultra-processed food, a regular sleep schedule, regular physical activity, and stress management - sound general health advice independent of homeopathic theory.

⚠
For diagnosed chronic conditions (diabetes, hypertension, thyroid disorders, heart or kidney disease), dietary management should be guided by your treating conventional doctor or a registered dietitian - Parhez advice here is complementary at most and should never contradict your primary doctor's advice.

Classical Dosing Conventions

Lower potencies (6C/30C, or the X scale) are conventionally associated with more physical/local, acute complaints and taken more frequently. Higher potencies (200C and above) are conventionally associated with constitutional prescribing and taken less frequently - sometimes a single dose followed by a waiting period. These are general conventions from classical texts and manufacturer packaging, not fixed rules - actual prescribing is the responsibility of a qualified homeopathic physician.

Safety Note

Remedies at high dilutions (12C and beyond) are generally regarded as containing negligible or no molecules of the original substance and are widely considered very low risk of direct toxic effect. However, mother tinctures and low potencies retain more original material and should be used strictly as labelled or as directed by a registered practitioner. Discontinue and seek medical advice if any unexpected reaction occurs.

Manufacturer Reference

SBL · Schwabe India · Dr. Reckeweg · Adel/Pekana

Authentic company history and product context, researched from official company sources.

SBL

History: Began operations in 1983 as Sharda Boiron Laboratories Ltd., a collaboration with Laboratoires Boiron of Lyon, France (a world leader in homeopathic manufacturing since 1932). Boiron assisted in establishing SBL's original manufacturing facility at Sahibabad, near Delhi (Ghaziabad, Uttar Pradesh).

Manufacturing: Facilities today include Sahibabad plus affiliate units at Jaipur, Haridwar (I & II), and Sikkim; registered office in Haridwar, Uttarakhand.

Products: Generics, single remedies, biochemic/combination remedies, mother tinctures, dilutions, specialty tablets, herbal products, and cosmetics.

Dr. Willmar Schwabe India

German Origins: Traces to Dr. Willmar Schwabe (1839-1917), who founded "Homöopatische Centralofficin Dr. Willmar Schwabe" in Leipzig in 1866, and published the landmark "Pharmacopoea Homoeopathica Polyglottica" in 1872.

India Operations: Incorporated 1994, production started 1997 at a dedicated plant in Noida, Uttar Pradesh - the Indian subsidiary of the German Dr. Willmar Schwabe Group, carrying its stated 160+ year manufacturing heritage.

Products: Single-remedy tablets, dilutions, mother tinctures, trituration tablets, LM potencies, biochemic tablets and bio-combinations, and specialty/complex formulations.

Dr. Reckeweg & Co. Germany

History: Traces to German physician Dr. Reckeweg's work in natural/homeopathic medicine, leading to Pharmazeutische Fabrik Dr. Reckeweg & Co. GmbH, Bensheim, Germany, founded 1947 - over 75 years of "Made in Germany" manufacturing, distributed in 40+ countries.

R-Series: Best known for numbered "R-series" drops (R1 through R89+) - complex formulations combining several homeopathic substances into one product targeted at a named complaint, distinct from classical single-remedy prescribing. Also manufactures single remedies, biochemic tablets, mother tinctures, and dilutions to German (DAB), European (Ph. Eur.), and Homeopathic Pharmacopoeia standards.

India Distribution: Imported directly from Germany, distributed through authorised regional stockists and pharmacies rather than manufactured domestically.

Adel / PEKANA

History: PEKANA Naturheilmittel GmbH, founded by pharmacist Peter and Katharina Beyersdorff in Kisslegg, Allgäu, Germany - first manufacturing permit from the Regional Council of Karlsruhe in 1975. Formulations included in the first German Homeopathic Pharmacopoeia (1991); received Baden-Württemberg's Innovation Prize (1995).

Spagyric Method: Formulations described as "homeopathic-spagyric" - combining classical potentisation with "spagyric" plant-extraction, a method with roots in alchemical tradition.

India: ADELMAR PHARMA GmbH (Forst, Germany) is the international marketing/distribution partner. In India, managed as Adel India / Adel Pharma, with over 45 years of Indian market experience. Product range centred on numbered complex drops (ADEL 1-87).

Regulatory Status & Scientific Evidence

An honest look at how homeopathy is regulated in India and what mainstream science says about it.

India's Regulatory Framework

Homeopathy is one of the recognised systems under the Ministry of AYUSH (Ayurveda, Yoga & Naturopathy, Unani, Siddha, Homoeopathy), established as a full ministry in 2003. The Central Council of Homoeopathy (CCH), a statutory body since 1973, regulated homeopathic education and practitioner registration; it was reconstituted as the National Commission for Homoeopathy (NCH) on 5 July 2021. BHMS (5.5 years including internship) is the recognised undergraduate qualification; MD (Homoeopathy) the recognised postgraduate qualification. Only BHMS/NCH-registered practitioners are recognised as qualified homeopathic physicians in India.

Scientific Evidence Position

The Australian NHMRC, after reviewing 225 controlled studies across 61 health conditions (published March 2015), concluded there are no health conditions for which there is reliable evidence that homeopathy is effective, and explicitly warned against using homeopathy for chronic, serious, or potentially serious conditions instead of treatments with good evidence of safety and effectiveness. The UK House of Commons Science and Technology Committee reached a similar conclusion in 2010: homeopathic products perform no better than placebo. Homeopathy advocacy organisations have disputed aspects of the NHMRC review's methodology - this remains a debated topic between homeopathy's professional bodies and mainstream evidence-review bodies, and this platform presents the mainstream scientific position.

What This Means Practically

  • Never use homeopathy as a substitute for vaccination
  • Never use it as a substitute for evidence-based treatment of serious, chronic, worsening, or emergency conditions
  • Always inform your conventional treating doctor about any homeopathic remedies you are taking
  • Consult a registered homeopathic physician (BHMS/NCH-registered) for any actual prescription rather than self-treating from reference information
India Context

Homeopathy in India

Scale, manufacturing, and research context.

Adoption and Scale

India has one of the largest homeopathic practitioner bases and patient populations in the world, formally integrated into the national AYUSH healthcare framework. Government homeopathic dispensaries and hospitals operate in many states alongside a very large private-practice sector.

Manufacturing Hub

India hosts substantial domestic manufacturing capacity (SBL and Dr. Willmar Schwabe India, both with large modern plants in the Delhi NCR region - Sahibabad/Ghaziabad and Noida respectively) alongside imported German brands (Dr. Reckeweg, Adel/Pekana) distributed through authorised Indian channels.

Research and Awareness

The Central Council for Research in Homoeopathy (CCRH), under the Ministry of AYUSH, is India's apex research body for homeopathy, conducting clinical research and drug proving studies, and organising awareness events around World Homeopathy Day (10 April) each year, marking Hahnemann's birth anniversary.
